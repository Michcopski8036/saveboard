## Why the search was thin

This environment's `WebFetch` tool returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED` for **every** external
domain I tried, without exception: reddit.com, apps.apple.com, play.google.com,
producthunt.com, trustpilot.com, g2.com, alternativeto.net, justuseapp.com,
threads.com, clien.net, 82cook.com, brunch.co.kr, news.ycombinator.com, even
en.wikipedia.org and techcrunch.com. That's an organization-level egress policy in
this session, not a per-site failure — per `/root/.ccr/README.md`, the right move on a
policy block is to report it, not route around it, so I didn't try mirrors or caches.

Separately, **Reddit itself is unreachable to Anthropic's web-search crawler** (a
`WebSearch` call scoped to `reddit.com` returned "not accessible to our user agent") —
so the single source the brief names first was categorically off-limits this run, by
any method, not just this session's proxy.

Everything above came from `WebSearch`'s own synthesis, which paraphrases far more
often than it quotes — most queries returned zero verbatim, single-source-attributable
sentences. The 6 above are the ones I could tie to one specific quoted sentence and one
specific URL with real confidence. I dropped several near-misses (a paraphrased Raindrop
"POOR RESPONSE FROM CUSTOMER SERVICE" complaint I couldn't re-derive with a clean
source; a Korean "반 파인트" bookmark-hoarding aside that turned out to be from
PeriodVol's own research, not SaveBoard's) rather than force them in.

**Korean yield was especially thin** — one hook, and it's adjacent rather than exact.
I did not find a real, sourceable 학부모 단톡방 / 맘카페 quote about a *lost link*
specifically. If this routine runs again, the highest-leverage fix is giving it a
research path that isn't fully dependent on `WebFetch` for social/forum content —
right now that tool can't reach any of the sites this pass is supposed to live on.
