## 2. "messages dissappearing Notifications not coming through, posts not sticking"

- **Source:** TeamSnap app review, aggregated at https://appgrooves.com/app/teamsnap-sport-team-management-by-teamsnap-inc/negative (original App Store/Play review, exact date not shown on the aggregator page).
- **Where from:** App-store review of TeamSnap (youth sports team-management app), via AppGrooves aggregator.
- **Persona:** Coach / team parent.
- **Format fit:** Shortform opener framing the *problem* team apps have (unreliable delivery) before pivoting to "so once you finally get the link, put it somewhere that won't lose it."
- **Feature flag:** **Important — this is a complaint about message/notification delivery, not about losing links after the fact.** SaveBoard does not send, receive, or guarantee delivery of messages — it's a save-from-share-sheet link organizer. Don't build a hook that implies SaveBoard fixes team-chat notification reliability. Use it only to set the scene ("team apps already lose your messages") before pivoting to what SaveBoard actually does (a permanent home for the link once you have it).

## Honesty note on search conditions and gaps

- **The fetch tool was non-functional for this entire pass.** Every attempted direct page fetch failed with HTTP 403 — not just bot-protected sites like Reddit, the App Store, Google Play, Facebook, Trustpilot, G2, JustUseApp, Chrome Web Store, Product Hunt, Brunch, Medium, and Threads, but also plain sites like a personal WordPress blog and Wikipedia. The only URL that fetched successfully all session was a raw GitHub text file. This means every "quote" above is a search-engine snippet, not a page I personally opened and re-read. I've flagged confidence accordingly and left out anything that only ever showed up as paraphrase.
- **I did not find a genuine, verbatim, individually-sourced quote of the exact scenario the brief opens with** — a parent saying something like "I can never find that link the coach sent." I ran many query variations in both English (r/Mommit, r/AskParents, "team mom," "resend the link," "scroll back through the group chat") and Korean (학부모 단톡방, 밴드, 학원 단톡방, combined with 링크/못찾/잃어버려/찾기 힘들) and got either how-to content (how to rejoin a KakaoTalk chat, how to recover deleted messages), news coverage of group-chat drama/bullying, or generic app-comparison content — never an individual's own sentence about losing a specific link. This is very likely a real, common thing people say (it's the premise of several existing team-management and family-organizer apps), but I could not source a real one to quote this week, so I'm reporting that gap rather than writing a quote that sounds right and presenting it as found.
- **The Korean side is the thinnest part of this batch** — 1 of 7 hooks, and it isn't even a complaint (it's satisfied-user praise for an unspecified bookmark app). Naver blog, Daum cafe, and Brunch — the places 학부모 단톡방 complaints would actually live — were all unreachable by the fetch tool, and WebSearch's Korean summaries consistently paraphrased rather than surfaced quotable sentences. A future pass with working page-fetch access to Naver/Daum/Brunch would likely do much better here.
- **None of the 7 hooks came from SaveBoard's own app-store reviews** — search turned up nothing indexed under "SaveBoard" reviews (small app, unsurprising), and Google Play / App Store listing pages couldn't be fetched directly to check manually.
- Every hook above that's flagged as "competitor marketing copy" (#5, #6, #7) is real, published, quotable text — but it's a rival product's own pitch, not a spontaneous user complaint. Worth having in the file as sourced vocabulary, but downstream writers should not present these as "here's what a frustrated user said."
