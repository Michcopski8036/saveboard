# Hook research — 2026-07-19

## Honesty note on method (read this first)

This pass was done almost entirely through search-engine snippets, not direct page fetches. WebFetch returned HTTP 403 on nearly every content-rich source I tried: reddit.com and old.reddit.com (blocked outright — "unable to fetch"), apps.apple.com, Google Play, Trustpilot, G2, Product Hunt, Medium, Gothamist, GroupMe's own blog, Daum, TILNOTE, and a personal review blog (arslan.io). I could not open a single one of these pages directly to check punctuation/whitespace against the source.

What I did instead: ran the same quote through 2+ independent WebSearch queries and only kept things where the wording came back identical or near-identical both times. Where a "quote" only ever came back as the search tool's own summary/paraphrase (e.g. TeamSnap-adjacent "parking lot" and "we never know what is going on" parent quotes, which I could not pin to any real, findable source after two tries), I dropped it rather than print it as a verbatim quote. That's why some plausible-sounding material below is missing — I'd rather hand over fewer than smuggle in something I can't stand behind.

Net result: **8 real, sourceable hooks** (5 high-confidence verbatim quotes, 3 clearly labeled paraphrases). This is a decent haul but the search felt structurally hampered, not just thin — a lot of raw Reddit and Korean 맘카페/단톡방 chatter almost certainly exists on exactly this pain point and I simply couldn't get past the fetch wall to read it directly. Reddit in particular is a total blind spot this round.

No SaveBoard-brand reviews exist yet on any store (checked; "SaveBoard" collides with an unrelated NZ building-materials company in search, and neither Apple nor Google turned up review content for our own app).

## 7. Ex-Pocket user grief (paraphrase, no handle)

**Paraphrase, not verbatim:** Reddit users reacting to Pocket's July 2025 shutdown described feeling "gutted" and grieving losing the app, with at least one describing using Pocket as a student with very slow internet to download long articles to read later.

- **Source:** referenced via Upworthy's coverage of the shutdown — https://www.upworthy.com/pocket-saying-goodbye/ — which itself pulled Reddit reactions. I could not open Reddit directly (blocked) to get exact wording or confirm which subreddit, so I'm not quoting a specific sentence, and I'm deliberately omitting the Reddit username the search tool surfaced.
- **Persona:** ex-Pocket user / general saver
- **Format fit:** blog title for a Pocket-alternative piece ("Pocket is gone. Here's where your grief goes.") — but written in our own words since I don't have a clean verbatim quote to lead with.
- ⚠️ **Feature-gap flag:** Pocket was a *read-it-later* app (full-text article reading, offline reading, text-to-speech). SaveBoard is a visual link-saver, not a reading app — it doesn't strip/reformat article text for reading or do offline article caching. Don't let a Pocket-nostalgia hook imply SaveBoard replaces the *reading* experience Pocket users are grieving; it replaces the *saving/organizing* half, not the read-it-later half.

## 8. mymind's search doesn't always find what you saved

**Paraphrase, not verbatim** (I could not open the source blogs — arslan.io and jacob-robinson.com both 403'd — so I'm not printing this as a quote): a published review of mymind describes searching for a specific saved item ("Designing Virtual Worlds") and getting back unrelated results (a Tim Ferriss book, Azure docs, a Godard film), with exact saved titles/URLs sometimes not surfacing in search at all.

- **Source:** Jacob Robinson, "A Review of MyMind" — https://jacob-robinson.com/2022/09/05/a-review-of-mymind/ (also cross-posted to Medium under his byline). Named, public, professional review — not a private individual's personal post.
- **Persona:** general saver / ex-mymind user
- **Format fit:** competitor-complaint angle for a mymind-alternative landing page or blog post.
- ⚠️ **Feature-gap trap — important:** the underlying complaint is that mymind's *AI/semantic "search by meaning"* feature doesn't reliably work. **SaveBoard does not have AI/semantic search** (confirmed against the codebase — no embeddings/vector search present). Do not let this hook get rewritten into "unlike mymind, SaveBoard's smart search always finds it" — that promises a capability we don't have. The honest angle, if used at all, is closer to "we skip the AI-search gimmick and just show you the page visually so you recognize it by sight" — recognition over search. Flag for whoever scripts this.

- Own-app (SaveBoard) reviews — none exist yet on either store.

## Summary

- **8 real hooks** handed over (5 higher-confidence verbatim, 3 explicitly labeled paraphrase).
- **Gut check:** thin, but *tool-thin* rather than *well-thin* — WebFetch was blocked on essentially every review platform and on Reddit entirely, so this reflects what two rounds of WebSearch snippets could surface and cross-verify, not the true size of the available material. A re-run with working page fetches (or direct Reddit/App Store API access) would very likely surface more, especially for the coach/team-parent and community-organizer personas, which came up emptiest.
- **Feature-gap flags for downstream writers:**
  1. Hook #8 (mymind search) — do not imply SaveBoard has AI/semantic search. It doesn't.
  2. Hook #7 (Pocket nostalgia) — do not imply SaveBoard replaces Pocket's read-it-later/offline-reading experience. It's a visual saver, not a reader.
