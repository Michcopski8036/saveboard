# Hook research — 2026-08-10

**8 hooks found** (target was 10 — reporting honestly rather than padding). English and Korean were both thinner than expected, for a structural reason, not a topical one: this session's web tools could not reach Reddit at all (search backend returns zero reddit.com results, and reddit.com is explicitly blocked to the crawler) and **WebFetch could not reach any page** in this environment (Apple App Store, Google Play, Threads, Quora, Product Hunt, G2, Trustpilot, justuseapp, Wikipedia, even example.com all came back `EGRESS_BLOCKED`). Every hook below was recovered through WebSearch snippets only — I could not open the source pages myself to re-verify wording character-for-character. Confidence is noted per hook. Nothing here is fabricated; where the search tool did not hand me a quotation-marked excerpt, I did not manufacture one.

**English search: thin.** Reddit — the single best predicted source for this brief — was completely unreachable. Mumsnet, BabyCenter, and PTA-forum searches surfaced thread titles but not quotable body text. Most "hook-shaped" English content that did surface was competitor content-marketing copy (SEO blog posts from other bookmark-tool vendors, e.g. contextbolt.com, linkamatic.com, shelfy.today) rather than real user voice — I excluded those on purpose since they're marketing copy, not the "real people" this brief wants.

**Korean search: also thin, but for a different reason.** Korean-language results were dominated by KakaoTalk/BAND how-to content and by other Korean bookmark-app SEO content marketing (링픽, 링크풀, 링포미, 딸깍, 링구 등) rather than organic complaints. I did not find a genuine 학부모 단톡방 (parent group-chat) or 코치 (coach) post describing lost-link frustration in their own words that I could verify well enough to quote — several searches aimed at that exact persona came back empty or off-topic (TikTok trend hashtags, app listings). That persona gap is real and worth another pass with actual Reddit/Naver-cafe/DC-inside access.

## Product-signal flags (collected across all hooks — read before writing copy)
- **Browser extension expectation** (Hook 6): people expect/complain about a browser extension experience when saving links. Confirm what SaveBoard actually offers on desktop web before implying "just like Raindrop's extension."
- **Per-item email sharing / share-notification expectation** (Hook 5): the complaint was about invited collaborators never getting a share email. If SaveBoard's board-sharing doesn't email invitees, don't imply it does.
- **"Folders" and "folder icons" vocabulary** (Hook 7): competitor users think in "folders" with custom icons, not "boards"/"categories." A hook borrowed from this vocabulary risks over-promising customization SaveBoard's board/category model doesn't have. Flag for writers: don't let "folder" language slip into SaveBoard copy if the product doesn't support per-folder custom icons.
- **KakaoTalk "favorite chat" behavior** (Hook 3) is about finding the *chat room* again, not a *link inside* a chat — a close but not identical pain point to SaveBoard's link-inside-chat use case. Don't present it as if it's literally about losing a shared link.
