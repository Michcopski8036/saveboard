## 1. The payment bug nobody could pay through

**Traced to:** commit `e960433` — "Android upgrade screen: stop showing the Apple IAP
view" (2026-08-13), and `CHANGELOG.md`'s iOS 1.0.9/Android 1.0.14 entry, which dates
the bug's start to 2026-05-27.

**Post:**
> Found and fixed a bug this week where Android users literally could not pay me.
>
> Since May 27, the upgrade screen on Android was showing the *iOS* payment view —
> Apple's in-app-purchase screen, which has no Android backend. Prices never loaded.
> There was no purchase button. Every Android user who tried to go Pro for ~2.5 months
> hit a dead end and (I assume) just left.
>
> Root cause: one `isNativePlatform()` check that should've been platform-specific and
> wasn't. Fixed now — Android gets a plain-text pointer to web checkout instead (Play's
> billing policy won't allow in-app Stripe), iOS keeps Apple IAP, web keeps Stripe.
>
> The unglamorous part of solo-founding: the bug that costs you real money is rarely
> the interesting one to fix.

**Note for the founder:** this is a real, dated fact from your own CHANGELOG, but it's
also a somewhat unflattering one (2.5 months of broken Android payments). Your call
whether that honesty is the right note for this week's batch — I'd rather flag it than
soften the number.

## 2. The spinner that only rendered on one load out of three

**Traced to:** commit `198dddf` — "Don't spin forever when getSession() is slow"
(2026-08-11).

**Post:**
> Shipped a fix today that I only found by accident.
>
> I was screenshotting the SaveBoard landing page locally and noticed it only actually
> rendered on one out of every three page loads. The other two just sat on a spinner.
>
> Turned out the entire app — including the landing page, which needs no login at all
> — was gated behind one `supabase.auth.getSession()` call with no timeout and no
> catch. If that call was ever slow, every visitor just... waited. Forever, as far as
> the UI was concerned.
>
> Added an 8-second timeout that opens the gate regardless (a session that arrives
> late still signs you in normally). Tested the same local reload 5 times after the
> fix: 5 out of 5 rendered.
>
> The bug wasn't the tooling being flaky. The bug was mine.

## 3. Why "you have to sign in to see this board" was losing people

**Traced to:** commit `0fb9ae7` — "feat: viewer→member bridge — /team invite page
shows the board, one-click join" (2026-08-19).

**Post:**
> If you've ever gotten a SaveBoard invite link and hit a blank sign-in screen before
> you could even see what you were being invited to — sorry, that was on me, and it's
> fixed now.
>
> The old invite page demanded you create an account before showing a single link from
> the board. Reasonable guess: most people just closed the tab.
>
> Now the invite page shows the actual board first — read-only, right there — with one
> button to join if you want in. Signed-in visitors join with one tap; new visitors go
> through sign-in and land straight in the board they were invited to, instead of a
> generic "welcome" screen.
>
> The whole point of a shared board is showing someone what's in it. The old flow
> forgot that before asking for anything.
