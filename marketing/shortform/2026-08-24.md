# Shortform — 2026-08-24

Six scripts: 4 screen-demo, 2 relatable-text. 3 English, 3 Korean. Each is built on a
hook from `marketing/hooks/2026-08-24.md`. Screen-demo scripts describe recordings of
the real app — verified against `src/app/App.tsx` (share-into-app flow via
`send-intent`/iOS ShareExtension, `fetchMetadata()` auto-fill), `CleanupModal.tsx`
(duplicate/broken-link finder), and `BoardShareModal.tsx` (invite + public link).
Reminders and Cleanup are **Pro** features — say so on screen where relevant.

---

## 1. [Screen-demo, EN] "10,000 bookmarks and I can't find anything"

**Built on hook #1** (Raindrop App Store review).

**On-screen text (paste-ready):**
1. Opening card: `"Saving is easy. Finding it again is the problem."`
2. Over the KakaoTalk/Instagram share sheet: `sharing a link into SaveBoard`
3. Over the Add modal auto-filling: `title, image, board — done`
4. Over the board view: `find it by the picture, not the memory`
5. Closer: `SaveBoard — save it so you can actually find it`

**What to record:**
- From any app (Safari, Instagram, KakaoTalk), tap Share → SaveBoard. Show the Add
  modal opening pre-filled with the title and thumbnail (this is real —
  `fetchMetadata()` in `App.tsx` does this automatically, no typing).
- Pick a board, tap save.
- Cut to a board with a handful of saved links shown as visual cards (favicon/image
  tiles) — the point is you're scanning pictures, not a wall of identical blue links.
- ~15-20s, vertical, no face needed.

## 2. [Screen-demo, EN] The Notion database nobody opens again

5. Closer: `SaveBoard — nothing to set up before you can save`

**What to record:**

  (can be any placeholder screen, doesn't need to be the founder's real Notion — the
  point is genre-recognition, not a specific document).

  it's just saved. Show the save happening in under 3 seconds start to finish.

  is meant to be fast to shoot back-to-back with script 1.

## 3. [Screen-demo, KO] 단톡방에서 온 링크, 못 찾을 때

**Built on hook #6** (Threads post about losing things in the KakaoTalk chat list).
Adapts the hook honestly — the quote is about losing a *chat room*, this script is
about losing a *link inside* one, which is the closer, more common version of the
same feeling for SaveBoard's actual persona (학부모/동호회 단톡방 운영자).

**화면 자막 (그대로 붙여넣기 가능):**
1. `"단톡방에 링크가 왔는데, 다음 날 찾으려니 없다."`
2. `"위로 계속 스크롤... 대체 어디 있지?"`
3. (공유 시트 화면) `"이제는 그 자리에서 저장"`
4. (SaveBoard 보드 화면) `"나중엔 여기서 바로 찾으면 돼"`
5. 마무리: `SaveBoard — 단톡방 링크, 잃어버리지 않게`

**촬영할 것:**
- 카카오톡 대화방에서 링크가 있는 메시지를 길게 눌러 공유 → SaveBoard 선택.
- Add 모달이 제목/썸네일 자동으로 채워지는 걸 보여주기 (타이핑 없음).
- 보드에 저장된 화면으로 컷 — 카드 여러 개가 이미지로 보이는 화면.
- 세로, 15-20초. 얼굴 없이 화면 녹화 + 자막만으로 충분.

## 4. [Screen-demo, KO] 보드 하나를 단톡방 전체에 공유하기

**Built on the SaveBoard-specific "coaches and community organisers" persona named in
the brief, not a single hook** — flagging that honestly since it isn't hook-anchored
the way the others are. Grounded in real code: `BoardShareModal.tsx`'s "Public link"
tab generates a shareable snapshot of a board.

**화면 자막:**
1. `"동호회 맛집 리스트, 나만 갖고 있으면 의미 없잖아"`
2. (Share 모달, Public link 탭) `"보드 하나를 링크 하나로"`
3. `"단톡방에 링크 하나만 붙여넣으면 끝"`
4. 마무리: `SaveBoard — 내 보드를 그대로 공유`

**촬영할 것:**
- 링크 여러 개가 담긴 보드를 연 상태에서 Share 버튼 → BoardShareModal 열기.
- "Public link" 탭으로 전환, 링크 복사.
- (연출) 그 링크를 카카오톡 단톡방에 붙여넣는 장면.
- **주의:** 이 보드의 소유자만 role 관리가 가능하고 초대 기능은 Team 플랜에 걸려
  있을 수 있음 — 정확한 플랜 게이팅은 촬영 전에 실제 화면에서 확인할 것
  (`BoardShareModal.tsx`의 `ownerPlan` prop 참고). 무료 플랜에서도 되는지 대표님이
  직접 로그인해서 확인 후 자막 문구를 조정해주세요.

## 5. [Relatable-text, EN] The read-later graveyard

**Built on hook #4** (dev.to, "Your 'Read Later' list is a graveyard").

No screen recording — text on a static or slow-moving shot (a phone face-down on a
desk, a hand scrolling a long list, whatever's fastest to shoot). Voice-over or
on-screen text only, no app footage.

**Script:**
> Your "read later" list is a graveyard.
>
> You know the one. Forty-three tabs. A bookmarks folder you haven't opened since
> March. A Pocket account that doesn't exist anymore.
>
> None of it was ever going to hurt you to save. The hurt part is that you can't
> find any of it again when you actually want it.
>
> That's the whole problem SaveBoard is for — not saving more, finding it again.

**Closing line to camera/text:** `Do you actually go back and read your "read later" list?`
(question format, invites a comment — matches the pattern PeriodVol's own
`marketing/story-bank.md` uses successfully for this format.)
