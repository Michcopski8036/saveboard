# Stop Losing Important Links in Group Chats — 5 Simple Habits

A registration form. A training schedule. A payment link. A permission slip.

These are the links that matter — the ones with deadlines, the ones that need action. And they're the ones most likely to get buried in a group chat and never found again.

Here are five simple habits that will stop this from happening.

---

## Habit 1: Save Links the Moment You See Them

The number one mistake people make: "I'll come back to that later."

Later never comes. Or rather, later arrives and the link is 300 messages deep.

The fix: save the link the instant you see it. This takes three seconds. Long-press the link → Share → SaveBoard. Done. The link is safe, organised, and searchable. You never need to find it in the chat again.

**The rule:** If a link matters, save it now. Not later.

---

## Habit 2: Use Collections, Not Folders

Most people's approach to organisation involves folders — and folders quickly become a mess of sub-folders that nobody can navigate.

Collections work differently. Think of them like topics or activities, not filing cabinets:

- **Basketball** — everything related to the basketball group
- **School** — newsletters, forms, event links
- **Recipes** — things to cook
- **Work** — client links, project resources

Flat, simple, quick to browse. When you save a link, assign it to a collection in one tap. When you need it later, open the collection and it's right there.

---

## Habit 3: Add a One-Line Note for Links with Deadlines

A link by itself doesn't tell you what you need to do with it. Add a note when you save it.

Examples:
- "Registration due June 30 — fill in for all three kids"
- "Pay this before Thursday"
- "Check this before the parent meeting"

One line. Takes five seconds. Means you'll never open a link and forget why you saved it.

---

## Habit 4: Review Your Saved Links Once a Week

Set aside five minutes every Sunday to open your link app and scan what's there.

This does two things:

1. **Catches deadlines** — you'll spot anything urgent before it becomes a problem
2. **Clears the clutter** — delete links you've already acted on so your collections stay clean

Five minutes a week beats an hour of panic the night before a deadline.

---

## Habit 5: Search Before You Ask

"Can someone send that link again?"

This question appears in almost every group chat at some point. Before you send it, check your link app first. If you've been saving links consistently, the answer is almost always there.

This habit alone will save you embarrassment — and save the group coordinator from having to resend things for the fifth time.

---

## Building the Habit

The hardest part of any organisational system is building the habit in the first place. Here's what works:

**Start with one group chat.** Pick the busiest one — probably the school or sport group — and save every link from it for one week. By day seven, it'll feel automatic.

**Keep the app on your home screen.** Friction is the enemy of habit. If the app is one swipe away, you'll use it. If it's buried in a folder, you won't.

**Celebrate the first win.** The first time you need a link and find it in SaveBoard instantly — that's the moment the habit sticks. It usually happens within the first week.
