## Why look for a Raindrop alternative?

Raindrop is powerful, especially on desktop with its browser extensions and nested collections. But common reasons people look for an alternative are:

- **The mobile experience is secondary.** Raindrop's strength is the web app and browser extensions; saving and browsing on a phone is less smooth.
- **It can feel heavy** if you just want to save links quickly, not manage a deep nested library.
- **It's not built around the share sheet.** The way most people save links today is one tap from inside another app — Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp, a browser.

## What makes SaveBoard a good Raindrop alternative?

SaveBoard is built for the mobile-first, share-sheet way people actually save links now.

- **Mobile-first** — native iOS and Android apps. Saving from the share sheet in any app takes one tap.
- **Visual cards** — every link becomes a card with its title, description, and preview image, so you can see what something is at a glance instead of scanning a list.
- **Boards, not nested folders** — simple boards you can drag to reorder and search instantly. No deep hierarchy to maintain.
- **Shareable boards** — send a whole board to someone with a link.
- **Free to start** — up to 30 links free, Pro is $3.49/month.
