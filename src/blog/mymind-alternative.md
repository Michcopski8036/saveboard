# The Best mymind Alternative With a Free Plan (2026)

mymind is a beautifully designed save-everything app — screenshots, images, links, notes — organised automatically by AI instead of folders. It's excellent for design inspiration and visual moodboards. But it has no free plan, and its AI-first approach means less control over how things get organised.

If you want a visual bookmark app you can actually try for free, and you mainly save **links** rather than screenshots and mood boards, **SaveBoard** is worth a look.

## Why look for a mymind alternative?

mymind is well-loved, but a few things send people looking elsewhere:

- **No free plan.** mymind is subscription-only, starting at $4.99/month — there's no free tier and no ongoing trial, so you can't try it without paying.
- **AI organisation isn't for everyone.** mymind auto-tags and sorts everything for you with no folders. That's convenient, but if you want to decide where something lives, it can feel like a black box.
- **It's built for all kinds of content** — screenshots, quotes, images, notes — which can feel like more than you need if you're mainly saving links to read or share later.

## What makes SaveBoard a good mymind alternative?

SaveBoard is a simpler, mobile-first tool focused specifically on saving and organising **links**.

- **Free to start** — up to 30 saved links and 5 boards at no cost, with no trial clock running out.
- **Mobile-first** — native iOS and Android apps, built around saving from the share sheet in one tap.
- **Visual cards** — every saved link becomes a card with its title, description, and preview image.
- **Boards you control** — you decide what goes where and can drag to reorder, instead of relying on AI sorting.
- **Shareable boards** — send a whole board to someone with a link.
