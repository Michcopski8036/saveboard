# The Busy Sports Parent's Guide to Organising Team Links

If you have kids in sport, your phone is probably drowning in group chats.

Basketball on Monday nights. Gymnastics Thursday and Saturday. Tennis every weekend morning. Each activity has its own parent group, its own coordinator, and its own stream of links — schedules, registration forms, payment pages, YouTube videos, permission slips.

Managing all of this shouldn't require a second job.

## The Multi-Sport Parent Problem

Here's what a typical week looks like across three kids in different sports:

**Monday:** Basketball coach shares the Term 3 training schedule PDF  
**Tuesday:** Gymnastics group posts the competition registration form (closes Friday)  
**Wednesday:** Tennis coordinator shares a YouTube video of a drill technique  
**Thursday:** School sends a permission form for the sports carnival  
**Friday:** Basketball group shares a payment link for new uniforms  

That's five links in five days across five different group chats. Miss any one of them and you're chasing your tail at 11pm before a deadline.

## Why Most Parents Fail at This

The problem isn't organisation skills — it's tools. Group chat apps are designed for messaging, not link management. The moment a link is shared, it starts getting buried.

Common coping strategies that don't quite work:

**The Screenshot Method:** Your camera roll becomes a graveyard of screenshots you can never find again.

**The Star/Pin Method:** WhatsApp and iMessage let you star messages, but the starred folder becomes cluttered fast and has no visual context.

**The Notes App Method:** Copy links into Apple Notes or Google Keep. Works until you have 50 links with no images, no organisation, and no quick way to search.

**The "I'll Remember" Method:** You won't.

## A Better System: One Place for Every Link

The fix is simple: one dedicated home for every important link from every group chat.

## Setting Up Your Collections

Here's a suggested collection structure for a sports family:

- **Basketball** — schedules, registrations, payment links, team contacts
- **Gymnastics** — competition forms, training videos, club notices
- **Tennis** — court bookings, coaching videos, tournament registrations
- **School** — newsletters, permission forms, event links
- **To Do** — anything with a deadline that needs action soon

Five collections, every link in its place. Takes ten minutes to set up, saves hours over the season.

## The Registration Form Problem

One of the most common pain points: registration forms with deadlines.

A form gets shared in the group chat. You mean to fill it in later. Later arrives and you can't find the link. You ask in the group. Everyone's embarrassed. You miss the deadline or the coordinator has to resend it.

With SaveBoard, add a note when you save it: "Due June 30 — all 3 kids" and assign it to the right collection. When you open the app, you see the form, your note, and the link. Done in thirty seconds.
