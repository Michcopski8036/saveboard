# How to Save Links from WhatsApp Group Chats (And Actually Find Them Later)

You're in a WhatsApp group chat. Someone shares a link — a registration form, a schedule, an important document. You think "I'll come back to that later."

Three days later, it's buried under 400 messages. Gone.

Sound familiar? You're not alone.

## Why Links Get Lost in Group Chats

WhatsApp is designed for conversation, not organisation. The moment a link is shared, the clock starts ticking. Here's what happens:

- **Day 1:** The link is easy to find, near the bottom of the chat
- **Day 3:** Buried under dozens of messages
- **Day 7:** Practically impossible to find without scrolling for minutes
- **Deadline day:** "Can someone send that link again?" — for the fifth time

For parents managing school groups, sports teams, and community chats, this happens dozens of times a week across multiple groups.

## The Old Ways Don't Work

**Screenshot it?** Screenshots pile up in your camera roll with no way to search them.

**Forward it to yourself?** Your "Saved Messages" folder becomes just as cluttered as the group chat.

**Copy the link into Notes?** Messy, unsearchable, no preview of what the link actually is.

There's a better way.
