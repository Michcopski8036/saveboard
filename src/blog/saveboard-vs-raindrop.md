# SaveBoard vs Raindrop.io: Which Bookmark Manager Should You Use? (2026)

SaveBoard and Raindrop.io are both modern bookmark managers — but they're built for different people. Raindrop is a desktop-first powerhouse for managing large libraries. SaveBoard is a mobile-first, visual saver for people who save links on the go.

Here's an honest, head-to-head comparison to help you pick.

---

## The short answer

- **Choose SaveBoard** if you mostly save links on your phone — from Instagram, YouTube, group chats, or your mobile browser — and want a fast, visual experience.
- **Choose Raindrop.io** if you work mostly on desktop, manage hundreds of bookmarks, and want nested collections plus browser extensions.

## Side-by-side comparison

| | SaveBoard | Raindrop.io |
|---|---|---|
| Primary platform | iOS & Android (mobile-first) | Web & desktop (browser-first) |
| Save from share sheet | ✅ One tap from any app | Limited |
| Visual cards | ✅ Title, description, image | Partial |
| Organisation | Simple boards, drag to reorder | Nested collections |
| Browser extensions | Coming 2027 | ✅ All major browsers |
| Full-text search | Basic | ✅ (Pro) |
| Shareable boards | ✅ | ✅ |
| Highlights / annotations | ❌ | Partial |
| Free tier | 30 links | Unlimited bookmarks |
| Paid price | $3.49/month | ~$3/month |
| Best for | Everyday mobile users | Desktop power users |

## Where SaveBoard wins

- **Mobile and the share sheet.** SaveBoard is built around saving from your phone in one tap. Raindrop's mobile app is secondary to its web app.
- **Visual at a glance.** Every link is a card with a preview image, so you recognise saves instantly instead of reading a list.
- **Simplicity.** Boards are quick to set up and keep tidy — no nested hierarchy to maintain.
- **Group chats and social.** SaveBoard is designed for the way people share links today — in WhatsApp groups, on Instagram, on YouTube.

## Where Raindrop.io wins

- **Desktop power.** Browser extensions across every browser and a polished web app.
- **Deep organisation.** Nested collections and tags for managing very large libraries.
- **Full-text search and highlights** on the Pro plan.
- **Unlimited free tier** for bookmark count.

We'd rather give you an honest comparison than pretend SaveBoard wins on everything — if you're a desktop researcher with thousands of bookmarks, Raindrop is genuinely great.

## Pricing

Both are affordable. Raindrop's free tier allows unlimited bookmarks; SaveBoard's free tier covers up to 30 links. Paid plans are similar — around $3–3.49/month — so price isn't the deciding factor. **How and where you save is.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is SaveBoard or Raindrop better?

Neither is universally "better" — they suit different users. SaveBoard is better for mobile-first users who save from the share sheet and want a simple, visual library. Raindrop.io is better for desktop power users who want nested collections, browser extensions, and full-text search.

### Is SaveBoard a good Raindrop.io alternative?

Yes, especially if you mainly save links on your phone. SaveBoard offers native iOS and Android apps, one-tap saving from the share sheet, and visual boards — areas where Raindrop's mobile experience is weaker.

### Which is cheaper, SaveBoard or Raindrop?

Both cost around $3–3.49 per month for their paid plans. Raindrop offers an unlimited free tier; SaveBoard's free tier covers up to 30 links. Pricing is similar, so choose based on platform and workflow rather than cost.

### Can I use SaveBoard on desktop?

SaveBoard is mobile-first, with native iOS and Android apps and your boards synced across devices. If your workflow is primarily desktop with heavy browser-extension use, Raindrop.io may suit you better.
