# Craft and Painting YouTube Channels for Kids

For rainy days, school holidays, and the afternoon that suddenly emptied. Ordered by how little you need to have in the house already.

## The five

1. **[The Best Ideas for Kids](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCuQZN7STmlGaDHaWHng-6AA)** (all ages · free) — **The shortest supply lists here.** Most of it starts with what is already in a kitchen drawer, which is why it travels so well between parents.

   There is a lot of it, and it is easy to pick by season. Run by Kimberly McLeod.

2. **[Red Ted Art](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCjjRFKvjpU1L1eDmfBWcqig)** (preschool–primary · free) — Maggy Woodley makes it. Bright, inventive, heavy on paper folding and turning packaging into something else.

   The instructions are short and clear enough for a child to follow without you.

3. **[Messy Little Monster](https://www.youtube.com/c/messylittlemonster)** (preschool · free) — Louise, a mother of three and an early years teacher, makes it. **This is the messy-play one.**

   Plenty of squishing, smearing and hands-in-it activities for younger children. The printables are free.

4. **[Babble Dabble Do](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCxcWYE_GFpLKyRFi_dzlkhQ)** (primary · free) — Ana Dziengel trained in architecture and industrial design, and it shows — a lot of what you make here **stands up and holds together.**

   Good for an older child who wants to know why it works.

5. **[Deep Space Sparkle](https://www.youtube.com/user/DeepSpaceSparkle)** (primary · free) — **This is the painting one.** Patty Palmer teaches it the way an art teacher would, in proper lessons.

   For the child who would rather paint than build.

## FAQ

### Are these channels free?

**Every channel here is free to watch on YouTube.** It is YouTube, so ads run. A few of them also sell an app or a membership separately, which is a different thing from the channel — where that applies, it is noted with the channel.

### What ages are they for?

It is noted with each channel. **Fewer channels state an age than you would expect** — where the makers do not say, this guide says so. Watching a few minutes together is still the most accurate way to decide.
