# Korean BBQ in Perth, Ranked: Which of the 9 Suits Your Table

Perth's Korean BBQ is concentrated in three pockets — the CBD, Northbridge and Victoria Park. The hard part isn't finding one, it's picking the right one for tonight: the room you want for eight loud friends is not the room you want for your parents.

The nine below are written from what thousands of diners keep repeating in their reviews. Not just the praise — where the same complaint comes up again and again, it's here too. Every name and address was re-checked against the venue's own listing (how the ranking and the checking work is in the FAQ).

## Pick by the night you're having

- **Someone at the table has never grilled before** — Namoo. Electric grills rather than charcoal, so nothing gets away from you.
- **Eight or more, and loud** — Tong 86. Warehouse-sized room, and about $30 a head.
- **Taking your parents** — Korean BBQ Village. The marinated beef ribs and the banchan are what people write about.
- **You want volume** — Seoul Buffet. $49 an adult, two hours, all-you-can-eat.
- **It's late and you're hungry** — Marubang. The Northbridge default after hours.
- **It's a warm evening** — Sura, for the big outdoor courtyard.

## The List

1. **[SSAM Korean BBQ & Mini Hot Pot](https://ssamkorean.com.au/)** ★ 4.3 · 1,017 reviews (313 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park WA 6100) — The most-reviewed place here, and the most consistent. Two things come up again and again: the banchan — kimchi, bean sprouts, potato salad — is refilled without asking, and the amount of food for the price is unusual. Reviewers tend to mention the meat quality and the attentiveness of the staff in the same breath. There's a per-person mini hot pot too, so the grill people and the soup people can share a table. The beef set for two is the easy start. $40–60pp
2. **[Namoo Korean Restaurant](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Namoo+Korean+Restaurant+78+Barrack+St+Perth)** ★ 4.3 · 745 reviews (78 Barrack St, Perth WA 6000) — A pork-belly specialist spread over three levels in the middle of the CBD, built around palsaik samgyeopsal: eight differently marinated pork bellies on one grill. Two things surface constantly in the reviews — the grills are electric rather than charcoal, which makes it forgiving for first-timers, and, unexpectedly, that the Korean fried chicken with cheese powder is the best in Perth. Regulars also mention how easy it is to book by phone. Dinner from 5pm. $40–60pp
3. **[Marubang](https://www.marubang.com.au/)** ★ 4.3 · 677 reviews (318 William St, Northbridge WA 6003) — Two venues at one address: noodles and braised pork downstairs, the BBQ upstairs. It's the name that comes up most for late Northbridge nights, and several reviewers report walking in on a Friday without a booking and getting a table for four. You order by QR code at the table and settle at the counter. The common order is a wagyu platter and rib fingers alongside pancakes and a soup. One group of six adults and two kids logged about $600 — worth knowing before a big night. $20–60pp
4. **[Korean BBQ Village Group](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Korean+BBQ+Village+361+Albany+Hwy+Victoria+Park)** ★ 4.3 · 491 reviews (361 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park WA 6100) — Overwhelmingly a family-occasion room. Mother's Day dinners come up repeatedly, and the marinated beef ribs get called tender in the same reviews that say every side dish was worth eating. Several long-time regulars write that it got better after the change of ownership, which is not the usual direction that sentence runs. The kitchen says it trims the meat after each order. It's also the only place here that opens at 11am, so it's the lunch option. $20–80pp
5. **[K Town Restaurant](https://www.google.com/maps/search/K+Town+Restaurant+63+Aberdeen+St+Northbridge)** ★ 4.2 · 846 reviews (63 Aberdeen St, Northbridge WA 6003) — An Aberdeen Street veteran, and the thing reviewers praise most isn't the meat — it's the kimchi, described more than once as the best they've had. The recurring complaints are worth knowing: near closing the kimchi station stops being refilled, and cuts are inconsistent (a tough rump, chicken not fully thawed). Go early in the evening. Dinner from 5pm. $40–60pp
6. **[Sura Korean Restaurant](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Sura+Korean+Restaurant+100+Francis+St+Northbridge)** ★ 4.1 · 881 reviews (100 Francis St, Northbridge WA 6003) — The old Sinabro merged into it, so regulars still use both names. The large outdoor courtyard is the point of this one, and BYO gets mentioned often. The interesting part: the non-grill dishes review better than the grill — several people call the jjajangmyeon the best in Perth. On the other side, the sweet and sour pork is inconsistent and the kimchi jjigae runs salty, so set your expectations on the noodles and the grill. $20–40pp
7. **[Tong 86](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Tong+86+86+Beaufort+St+Perth)** ★ 4.1 · 546 reviews (86 Beaufort St, Perth WA 6000) — Graffiti walls, K-pop videos, warehouse proportions: this is the group room. Reviewers repeatedly land on the same number — about $30 a head without drinks, and full. Food arriving fast is a common note. The recurring gripe is that a second round of meat sometimes arrives still frozen, so check what lands when you reorder. In the Ferguson Buildings. $20–40pp
8. **[Seoul Buffet](https://www.seoulbuffet.com/)** ★ 4.0 · 444 reviews (425 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park WA 6100) — All-you-can-eat cook-your-own BBQ plus steamboat, with a full bar, at $49 an adult for a two-hour sitting. The reviews converge on one thing: the fun is in choosing. Meat, seafood and vegetables you collect yourself, which suits a night when nobody wants to negotiate a menu. Value is the word that keeps appearing. $40–60pp
9. **[Arirang Korean BBQ Restaurant](https://arirang.com.au/)** ★ 3.8 · 968 reviews (91-93 Barrack St, Perth WA 6000) — A long-running CBD room built around a 500g combo set with sides. The reason its score sits lowest here is service rather than food: reviews describing a rude reception sit right next to ones describing a warm welcome. To its credit, the owner replies to the bad ones with an apology. Note also that its own website lists a Bayswater address while its Google listing says Barrack Street — both are reported here rather than one being chosen. $20–40pp

## FAQ

### What is the ranking based on?

Google rating, highest first, with review count breaking ties. We haven't eaten at all nine in the same week, so a score thousands of diners actually gave is a more honest basis than our opinion — and each write-up above is built from what those reviews keep repeating. It's a blunt measure: it favours places that have been around, and it says nothing about whether a room suits your table. That's what the "pick by the night" section above is for.

### How were these addresses verified?

Each venue's name, street address and suburb was read on 28 July 2026 from that venue's own Google Business Profile — the listing the business controls itself — rather than copied from another article. Published lists we checked had one of these restaurants on the wrong street number and another in a suburb it doesn't trade in. Where the venue also runs its own website, that was checked too, and any conflict is reported rather than smoothed over.

### Why nine and not ten?

Because only nine cleared the verification bar on the day. Padding a list to a round number is how the wrong-address problem starts.

### Are the ratings and prices current?

They were Google's figures on 28 July 2026, included to show scale rather than as a live feed. Ratings drift and prices rise — check before you book.
