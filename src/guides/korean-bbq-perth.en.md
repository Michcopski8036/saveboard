# Korean BBQ in Perth, Ranked: 9 Places With Verified Addresses

Most "best Korean BBQ in Perth" lists are copied from each other and quietly go stale. While putting this one together we found published lists placing one of these restaurants on the wrong street number, and another in a suburb it doesn't trade in — small errors, but the kind that send you to the wrong door on a Friday night.

**The ranking below is by Google rating, highest first, with review count breaking ties.** That's a blunt measure, and we're stating it rather than dressing it up as a critic's verdict: we haven't eaten at all nine in the same week, so a score thousands of Perth diners actually gave is a more honest basis than our opinion. A place at 4.3 with 1,017 reviews is a safer bet than one at 4.3 with 491, which is why review count is the tiebreak.

What we did do ourselves is the boring part: **every name, street address and suburb below was re-checked on 28 July 2026 against that venue's own Google Business Profile**, the listing the business itself controls. Where a venue also runs its own website, that was checked too. Nine places cleared it. We'd rather publish nine verified than ten with one guess in it.

## The List

1. **SSAM Korean BBQ & Mini Hot Pot** (313 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park WA 6100) — Google 4.3 from 1,017 reviews, the biggest review base on this list. Korean BBQ plus a per-person mini hot pot, so one table can do both. Own site at ssamkorean.com.au. $40–60pp.
2. **Namoo Korean Restaurant** (78 Barrack St, Perth WA 6000) — Google 4.3 (745). CBD pork-belly specialist, known for its palsaik samgyeopsal: eight differently marinated pork bellies on one grill. Dinner from 5pm, with takeaway and delivery listed. $40–60pp.
3. **Marubang** (318 William St, Northbridge WA 6003) — Google 4.3 (677). Two venues at one address, per the restaurant's own site: Korean noodles and braised pork on the ground floor, Korean BBQ upstairs. $20–60pp.
4. **Korean BBQ Village Group** (361 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park WA 6100) — Google 4.3 (491). Charcoal grills, and the only place on this list whose profile shows an 11am opening — so it's the lunch option. $20–80pp.
5. **K Town Restaurant** (63 Aberdeen St, Northbridge WA 6003) — Google 4.2 (846). Aberdeen Street standby for tabletop grilling, listed as LGBTQ+ friendly on its own profile. Dinner from 5pm. $40–60pp.
6. **Sura Korean Restaurant** (100 Francis St, Northbridge WA 6003) — Google 4.1 (881). Busy wood-clad room with tabletop grills and a large courtyard, which makes it one of the better warm-evening options. $20–40pp.
7. **Tong 86** (86 Beaufort St, Perth WA 6000) — Google 4.1 (546). Warehouse-sized room with graffiti walls and K-pop videos on the screens; the one to pick when the group is big and loud. In the Ferguson Buildings. $20–40pp.
8. **Seoul Buffet** (425 Albany Hwy, Victoria Park WA 6100) — Google 4.0 (444). All-you-can-eat cook-your-own BBQ and steamboat with a full bar; its own site lists a two-hour sitting at $49 per adult. $40–60pp.
9. **Arirang Korean BBQ Restaurant** (91-93 Barrack St, Perth WA 6000) — Google 3.8 (968). The lowest score here, but with nearly a thousand reviews behind it, and a long-running CBD room for traditional Korean BBQ. Note its own website lists a Bayswater address while its Google listing says Barrack Street — we report both rather than picking one. $20–40pp.

## FAQ

### How were these addresses verified?
Each venue's name, street address and suburb was read on 28 July 2026 from that venue's own Google Business Profile — the listing the business controls itself — rather than copied from another article. ### What is the ranking based on?
Google rating, highest first, with review count breaking ties between places on the same score. We state it plainly because it is a blunt measure: it reflects thousands of diners rather than one visit, but it also rewards volume and says nothing about whether a place suits your table.

### Why nine and not ten?
Because only nine cleared the verification bar on the day. Padding a list to a round number is how the wrong-address problem starts.
