# Drawing YouTube Channels for Kids

All these need is paper and a pencil. Your child follows the screen one step at a time and ends up with something finished.

## The four

1. **[Art for Kids Hub](https://youtube.com/artforkidshub)** (ages 5 to primary · free) — Rob draws beside his own children, and their attempts stay on screen next to his. That is the trick — a child sees a drawing they could actually manage.

   Animals, food, characters they already love: the library is enormous. Six family members make it. There is a paid membership, but YouTube is free.

2. **[Cartooning Club How to Draw](https://www.youtube.com/@cartooningclub)** (primary and up · free) — Superheroes, anime and game characters. **Best for a child who already has a favourite** they want to draw.

   It suits slightly older children — more lines, longer steps.

3. **[Draw So Cute](https://www.youtube.com/drawsocute)** (preschool–primary · free) — Exactly what the name says. Wennie runs it, and **free colouring pages** sit alongside the videos.

   Handy when a child wants to keep going after the drawing is done.

4. **[Draw With Rob](https://youtube.com/c/robbiddulph72)** (preschool–primary · free) — British picture-book author Rob Biddulph started this in the 2020 lockdowns, **made for a parent and child to watch together.**

   The pace is unhurried and the voice is kind, which is what a first-time follower-along needs.

## FAQ

### Are these channels free?

**Every channel here is free to watch on YouTube.** It is YouTube, so ads run. A few of them also sell an app or a membership separately, which is a different thing from the channel — where that applies, it is noted with the channel.

### What ages are they for?

It is noted with each channel. **Fewer channels state an age than you would expect** — where the makers do not say, this guide says so. Watching a few minutes together is still the most accurate way to decide.
