# Maths and Reading YouTube Channels for Kids

Numbers and letters do not have to start at a desk. Met first through a song or a story, they go down much easier. Three channels worth having.

## The three

1. **[Numberblocks and Alphablocks](https://www.blocksuniverse.tv/)** (preschool · official site) — The series where numbers are blocks and letters are characters. Counting, adding and phonics become something you can watch happen. It lands especially well with preschoolers.

   **This link goes to the official site rather than a YouTube channel** — the makers do not publish a channel address, so this is the accurate place to start.

2. **[Numberock](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCt9SZgFExNwWTH5T_JnyF-A)** (primary school · free) — Times tables and place value set to music. The kind a child hums for a week and then simply knows.

   The spelling trips people up: the brand is one word, **Numberock**.

3. **[Storyline Online](https://www.youtube.com/StorylineOnline)** (preschool–primary · free, nothing sold) — **Actors read picture books aloud** — names you know, Meryl Streep among them. Each book credits its reader.

   It is run by the SAG-AFTRA Foundation, so there is nothing to buy. Good at bedtime.

## FAQ

### Are these channels free?

**Every channel here is free to watch on YouTube.** It is YouTube, so ads run. A few of them also sell an app or a membership separately, which is a different thing from the channel — where that applies, it is noted with the channel.

### What ages are they for?

It is noted with each channel. **Fewer channels state an age than you would expect** — where the makers do not say, this guide says so. Watching a few minutes together is still the most accurate way to decide.
